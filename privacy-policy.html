Permissions We Use

To provide the best functionality, Secure Authenticator requests certain permissions:

    • Data Handling: Images are processed directly on your device and are never saved or sent to any external server.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET):
    • Purpose: Connects to the internet for updates and resources essential to the App.
    • Data Handling: No personal information is transmitted.
  • Network State Access (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE):
    • Purpose: Verifies network connectivity to ensure internet availability as needed.
    • Data Handling: Used solely to check connection status; no data is retained or distributed.
  • Vibration Control (android.permission.VIBRATE):
    • Purpose: Offers haptic feedback during certain App interactions.
    • Data Handling: Does not involve collecting or sharing any data.
